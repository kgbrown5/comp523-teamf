Measles Dashboard

Mapping measles vaccination coverage and making it easier for states to contribute data, in partnership with ACCIDDA.

Project Introduction

We are working with Dr. Carl Pearson at UNC's Atlantic Coast Center for Infectious Disease Dynamics and Analytics (ACCIDDA) to expand an interactive map of estimated measles vaccination coverage. The dashboard helps public health officials explore coverage by state, county, and school to identify communities with gaps in vaccination. Our team is making it easier for organizations in each state to contribute and update their own data, so the map can grow beyond its current coverage.

The Client

Dr. Carl Pearson

Atlantic Coast Center for Infectious Disease Dynamics and Analytics (ACCIDDA), UNC Epidemiology
