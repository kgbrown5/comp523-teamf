Team Rules

Team's behavioral rules, based on the course team rules template.

Communication

  • Aim to respond to all communication within 24 hours of outreach
  • Stakeholder communication via email, team communication via text
  • Meetings are primary form of communication; discuss decisions and updates in-person when possible.

Work Standards

  • Project code merged through PR, reviewed by at least one other team member
  • Communicate any issues with meeting deadlines in advance

Conflict Resolution

  • Handled first within team, brought to coach if necessary

Meeting Norms

  • Punctual attendance expected at all meetings
  • Note-taker designated before meeting
  • Agenda reviewed and shared with stakeholders ahead of meeting
